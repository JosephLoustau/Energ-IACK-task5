# Context

We want to generate a decision-making tool for strategic planning in the building sector. The idea is to decarbonize the building stock and to give guidelines to the city of Geneva, owner of 800 buildings.

The plan is to implement a web-app, built on python, that collect data on buildings and on legislations, objectives, etc to allow the decision-maker to assess where there are in terms of objectives that they can choose, when putting in place measures that they can choose as well. 

The idea is to have controls that they can play with, and results in a map where buildings are colored as a function on one tab and on another results in a cloud of points along objective axis, where we can situate the buildings according to the objectives zones.

Once they have decided on a strategy, the can download a report, most likely in html to keep interactive features, where the strategy is presented in terms of KPIs, budget, etc and then they'll get a proposition of operational setup for the strategy (meaning timelines of what is happening).
